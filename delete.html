Delete your Simple Lift account

Simple Lift is a workout log. This page is how you ask for the account on the Simple Lift server to be deleted, including from a phone where the app is no longer installed.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Simple Lift and go to Settings → Account & sync → Delete account.
  2. Enter your password and type DELETE.
  3. Confirm. The server then deletes that account.

This removes the email, password hash, synced plans, history, settings, the live workout snapshot, watch settings, watch sign-in tokens (including device id, model, name, and last seen), pairing codes, watch results waiting to sync, email codes, and that account's AI usage counts. Simple Lift data on the device you use is erased too.

What is not deleted

  • Short-lived rate-limit counters stored by IP address. They are not tied to the account and expire on their own within an hour.
  • The service-wide AI spend total, which is not tied to an account.
  • Emails already sent, anything you already sent to xAI with the AI features, and workouts saved on a watch or in Garmin Connect. Those stay with that service.

If you cannot sign in

Email simpleliftsupport@gmail.com from the account email and ask for the account to be deleted. Include the address the account uses. A person reads that mailbox and deletes the account. It is not an instant button, and the message has to come from the account email so the request can be matched.

To remove a copy on another phone or browser, sign in there and delete the account, or clear the site data for Simple Lift.
